Overview

Curated sample

Dandeli sits on the Kali river surrounded by the Dandeli Wildlife Sanctuary, part of the Kali Tiger Reserve. It is best known for white-water rafting, kayaking and jungle lodges. Hornbills and other forest birds are commonly seen, while larger animals are harder to spot.

How to reach

From Bangalore: about 470 km by road (8 to 9 hours). Hubballi, about 75 km away, has an airport and a major railway station; Alnavar is the nearest railhead.

Getting around

Most visitors stay at resorts or jungle lodges that arrange transfers and activities. Local taxis are available from Dandeli town.

Travel tips

  • Book rafting slots in advance on weekends
  • Carry insect repellent and quick-dry clothing
  • Keep noise low on safaris to improve sightings

Safety

Rafting requires life jackets and a briefing; follow the guides. Do not enter the river outside supervised activities, as currents change with dam releases.

Frequently asked

Is rafting in Dandeli safe for beginners?+

Operators run graded stretches with guides and safety gear. Age and health limits apply; check with the operator.

Will I see tigers?+

Tiger sightings are rare. Birds, deer and other wildlife are more commonly seen.
