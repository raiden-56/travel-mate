Overview

Curated sample

Murudeshwar sits on a small headland on the Arabian Sea, crowned by a large Shiva statue and a tall temple gopura. The beach is lively in the evenings. It is also the jumping-off point for scuba diving and snorkelling trips to Netrani Island in season.

How to reach

From Bangalore: about 490 km by road (9 to 10 hours). Murdeshwar station on the Konkan Railway is close to town, and overnight buses run from Bangalore. Mangaluru is the nearest major airport.

Getting around

The temple, statue and beach are within walking distance. Dive operators arrange boats to Netrani Island.

Travel tips

  • Book scuba trips in advance and confirm what the price includes
  • Visit the gopura viewpoint in the morning for clearer views
  • Dress modestly at the temple

Safety

Only dive with certified operators who provide a safety briefing and equipment checks. Avoid swimming where flags or lifeguards advise against it.

Frequently asked

Can beginners scuba dive at Netrani?+

Many operators offer introductory dives for non-certified divers with an instructor. Check health requirements before booking.

How long should I stay?+

One day covers the temple and beach; add a day for a Netrani dive trip.
